ABOUT MY THESIS

Research driven by real-world problems

As part of my PhD research, I am developing sampling simulation solutions for the mining sector, with a particular focus on urban mining and waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE).

My goal is to design decision-support tools that optimize sampling plans to achieve target uncertainty levels for metal content while minimizing operational costs. Ultimately, these methods are intended to be integrated into software solutions for industrial applications.
